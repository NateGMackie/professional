# AI Use in Technical Writing  

**Purpose**  
Define how AI tools can be used responsibly in technical writing, supporting efficiency and quality while ensuring authorship, accuracy, and compliance remain with the technical writer.  

---

## Acceptable Uses  

- **Draft Assistance**  
  - Use AI to brainstorm, outline, or suggest phrasing, tone, or structure.  
  - Use AI for proofreading, grammar, and readability improvements.  
  - Use AI to organize your own notes or SME notes.  

- **Internal Summaries / Private Use**  
  - Use AI to summarize vendor manuals, research, or other references strictly for internal understanding or personal notes.  

- **Style & Structure Support**  
  - Use AI to apply company style guidelines or tone of voice to draft text, provided the writer reviews and finalizes the document.  

- **Metadata & Organization**  
  - Use AI to suggest titles, summaries, tags, or content structure to support organization and consistency.  

---

## Uses Requiring Caution  

- **Documents for Internal or External Publication**  
  - AI-assisted drafts must always be reviewed, edited, and finalized by the technical writer.  
  - Publishing AI output “as-is” is not permitted.  

- **Vendor or Proprietary Materials**  
  - Do not use AI to generate publishable documents from vendor manuals or proprietary IP.  
  - Vendor materials may be referenced for context or summarized for personal notes, but final authored text must be original.  

---

## Ethical Safeguards  

- **Verification & Accuracy**  
  - Fact-check, edit, and validate all AI-assisted text before publication.  

- **Data Privacy**  
  - Never enter confidential, sensitive, or regulated information into public AI tools.  

- **Accessibility & Inclusivity**  
  - Review AI-assisted content for plain language, readability, accessibility, and inclusivity.  

---

## Accountability & Ownership  

- AI is considered a **supporting tool**—similar to spellcheck or Grammarly—not an author.  
- The technical writer is always the **sole author and owner of final deliverables**, regardless of AI use.  
- Responsibility for accuracy, quality, and compliance remains with the technical writer.
