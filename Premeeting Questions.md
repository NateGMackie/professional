# 🧠 Need clarity before we document?

Before I can write clear, useful documentation, I need to understand a few key things:

---

## 👀 What is this?

- What’s changing, launching, or being implemented?  
- What problem does it solve or goal does it support?  
- Is this replacing something?  
- Who owns this change?

---

## ⚙️ How does it work?

- What's the timeline (prep → launch → follow-up)?  
- What happens behind the scenes?  
- Who's involved at each phase?  
- Any blockers or risks?  
- What if this doesn’t happen?

---

## 🧩 What do people need to do?

- What will users need to do differently?  
- Are there new steps or tools to learn?  
- What UI elements (screens/buttons/fields) are involved?  
- What might trip people up?

---

## 📜 Are there rules?

- Is this tied to a policy or compliance rule?  
- Any restrictions or must-follow requirements?  
- What’s the official guidance or best practice?

---

## 🌍 Who’s impacted?

- Who’s affected (teams, systems, customers)?  
- What should they *not* do?  
- Is training or support already planned?

---

📌 Send what you know — even rough notes are helpful. I’ll shape it from there.
