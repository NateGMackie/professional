# How I Work: Documentation Process

This guide outlines what to expect when you work with me on documentation projects. Each stage is collaborative and focused on delivering clear, user-focused content.

---

## 1. Start: You identify the need

You (a business unit or project team) identify a documentation need and reach out to me.

**What to expect:**
- I’ll confirm scope and urgency.
- We’ll schedule a kickoff meeting.

---

## 2. Requirements gathering: We define the scope

In our initial meeting, we define the project together.

**During the meeting:**
- I walk through this documentation process.
- We identify the **target audience** and what they need to **know** or **do**.
- We create a **topic list** based on those needs.
- You identify the **SMEs** (subject matter experts) and **approvers**.

**Your role:**
- Bring key stakeholders or help me connect with them.
- Be ready to talk audience, goals, and must-have topics.

---

## 3. Discovery: SMEs share their knowledge

I gather information from the SMEs to build the documentation.

**Preferred sources include:**
- Process notes, wiki pages, chat/email threads
- Diagrams, screenshots, and annotated flows
- Existing job aids, SOPs, or training content

> ⚠️ Demos are not ideal as primary sources.  
> ✔ Written references are easier to work from and validate against.

**Your role:**
- Prioritize providing written or visual references.
- If no content exists, provide a structured walk-through after sending notes or screenshots.

---

## 4. Drafting: I write clear, modular content

I use the source material to create user-focused documentation.

**You’ll see content that is:**
- Modular and easy to scan
- Built around what users need to **know** and **do**
- Aligned to our style guide and Information Mapping principles

**Your role:**
- Respond to follow-up questions as I write.
- Grant access to the application, sandbox, or test environment if available—this helps me validate steps and improve accuracy.

---

## 5. Review and edit: You validate the content

SMEs review the draft and give feedback. Once SME approval is complete, I submit it to the final approvers.

**You’ll receive:**
- A draft in your preferred format
- Clear guidance on what kind of feedback is helpful
- A deadline for review

**Your role:**
- Provide timely, actionable, and specific feedback.
- Help route the document to the right approvers if needed.

---

## 6. Publish and socialize: We launch the docs

Once approved, I publish the documentation to the appropriate platform (SharePoint, Confluence, ServiceNow, etc.).

**I also:**
- Draft communications (email, intranet post, etc.) to notify your audience
- Share how users can access and use the content

**Your role:**
- Help select communication channels
- Reinforce the message with your team or audience if needed

---
